Dino Ge Zhang is a media anthropologist and non-fiction writer. His research focuses on socio-technics, aesthetics, and affective ecologies of the Sinophone internet. He is the founding editor of the [Bureau of Low Theory](https://lowtheory.org), a web database dedicated to internet culture and underground theory from China.
